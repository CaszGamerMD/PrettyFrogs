# PrettyFrogs Wiki

Welcome to **PrettyFrogs**, a Fabric mod for **Minecraft Java Edition 26.2** by Casz. Transform adult vanilla frogs into themed cosmetic variants, give them accessories, and customize how the frogs look on your own client.

> **Version covered:** current `main` branch, including the Field Guide crafting recipe, Tadpole Costume, Ghost/Skeleton model upgrades, and Field Guide appearance settings. Features described here are implemented in the source; visual appearance still benefits from in-game testing.

## Three steps to your first custom frog

1. Install Minecraft **26.2**, the matching **Fabric Loader**, **Fabric API**, and the PrettyFrogs mod JAR.
2. Find or summon an **adult** frog.
3. Right-click it with an item from [[Frog Forms]], such as a **Melon Slice**, **Bone**, or **Tadpole Bucket**.

To undo a custom form, use a **Milk Bucket** on the frog. The frog's underlying vanilla biome variant remains intact.

## The frog book

Craft the **PrettyFrogs Field Guide** from a **Book + Tadpole Bucket** (shapeless). Its **Frogs** pages show form previews; its **Appearance** page holds client-only HD texture and extra 3D decoration settings.
